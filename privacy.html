Summary

MCTiers Rebirth stores only what it needs to run testing queues, tickets and a tierlist for each Discord server. That is your Discord user ID, the Minecraft account you link, and your testing history. It does not read or store the content of your messages. It does not sell your data or use it for advertising.

What we store

Everything is stored per Discord server. Data from one server is not shared with or shown in another.

What we do not store

  • Message content. The bot notices when a message is sent in an open testing ticket, so that active tickets are not auto-closed. It records only the time, never what was written.
  • Your Discord username, email, IP address, avatar or other profile details. The bot shows your name by mentioning your user ID, and Discord displays it.
  • Microsoft, Xbox or Minecraft sign-in tokens. If you verify with Microsoft, you sign in on Microsoft’s own website. The bot uses the resulting tokens once to read your Minecraft username and UUID, then discards them.
  • Passwords or Minecraft login details. Linking only needs a public Minecraft username. Microsoft sign-in happens on Microsoft’s own website; the bot never sees your password.

Messages the bot posts

Queue panels, ticket messages and test results are posted as normal Discord messages in the server’s channels. Those messages are stored by Discord and controlled by the server, under Discord’s Privacy Policy. Testing tickets are deleted when they are closed.

Outside services

To do its job, the bot sends a small amount of data to these public services:

  • Mojang (api.mojang.com, api.minecraftservices.com): the Minecraft username you enter, to check it exists and get its UUID.
  • Microsoft, Xbox Live and Minecraft services (login.microsoftonline.com, xboxlive.com, api.minecraftservices.com): only if you choose to verify with Microsoft, to confirm which Minecraft account you own.
  • MCTiers (mctiers.net): a Minecraft username or UUID, to show official MCTiers rankings and badges on profiles and in tickets.
  • mc-heads.net: a Minecraft UUID is part of the player-head image link in embeds. Discord loads that image.

No Discord IDs are sent to these services. They have their own privacy policies.

How long we keep it

Queue entries are removed when you leave the queue or your ticket opens. Ticket records are removed when the ticket closes. Cooldowns expire on their own. Account links, ranks, link history and test history are kept as long as the bot runs in that server, because they make up the server’s tierlist and records, or until they are deleted as described below. If the bot is removed from a server, that server’s data can be deleted on request.

Your choices and deleting your data

  • Leave the queue at any time with /leave.
  • Unlink your account or remove your ranks: ask the staff of that server. They can use /forceauth unlink and /tierwipe.
  • See or delete everything stored about you: contact the bot owner (below) with your Discord user ID and the server. We will reply within 30 days. Some test records may be kept in anonymised form, with your Discord ID removed, so a server’s tester statistics stay correct.

Who can see your data

Your rank, peak tier and linked Minecraft name can be seen by anyone in the server, for example through /profile. Account link history, tester statistics and settings are only shown to that server’s testers or staff. The bot owner can access the database to run and maintain the bot. We never sell or rent your data.

Security

Data is kept in a database on the machine that runs the bot, which is not publicly reachable. No system is perfectly secure, but we only store the limited data listed above.

Children

The bot is meant for people who meet Discord’s minimum age. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone younger.

Changes

This policy may change. The date at the top shows the latest version. Important changes will be announced where possible.

Contact
